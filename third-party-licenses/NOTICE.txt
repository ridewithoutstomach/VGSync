KVRouite - Notice for the remaining bundled components
================================================================================

Two other folders carry legal texts of their own and are not repeated here:

  _internal/gstreamer   GStreamer, GES, PyGObject and the plugin libraries
  _internal/qt          Qt 6, PySide6 and shiboken6

This folder covers what is left: the Python runtime and the smaller libraries
that the Windows build contains. All of them are under permissive licenses.

installed by the user.
