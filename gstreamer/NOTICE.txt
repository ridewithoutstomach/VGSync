 NOTICE & SOURCE CODE OFFER
 GStreamer / GStreamer Editing Services
========================================

This folder documents the GStreamer components that KVRouite distributes as
part of the Windows build.

The binaries themselves are NOT in this folder. On Windows they are installed
by pip (package "gstreamer-bundle", version 1.28.6) and are placed by the
build process into the "_internal" folder next to KVRouite.exe, in the
sub-folders "gstreamer_libs", "gstreamer_plugins", "gstreamer_plugins_libs",
"gstreamer_plugins_restricted", "gstreamer_plugins_gpl",
"gstreamer_plugins_gpl_restricted", "gstreamer_python",
"gstreamer_ext_runtime" and "gi".

On LINUX nothing of this is distributed with KVRouite. GStreamer comes from
the distribution's own packages (python3-gi, gir1.2-ges-1.0,
gstreamer1.0-plugins-*), installed by the user. This notice then only serves
as attribution.

----------------------------------------
 Modifications
----------------------------------------
KVRouite does not modify GStreamer. The binaries are redistributed unchanged
as they are published in the "gstreamer-bundle" wheels for version 1.28.6.
KVRouite uses GStreamer through its public API (GES, GstPbutils, GstVideo)
via the PyGObject bindings.

----------------------------------------
 Replacing the libraries (LGPL section 6)
----------------------------------------
All GStreamer libraries are shipped as separate, dynamically loaded DLLs in
the "_internal" folder. They are not linked statically into KVRouite.exe and
are not compressed into a single-file executable. You may replace them with
your own build of the same libraries: overwrite the DLLs in the corresponding
"_internal/gstreamer_*" sub-folder and restart the application.
